Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026 · DealTough is operated by Doer Tough LLC.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address. Your password is stored only as a bcrypt hash — we cannot read it.
  • The deals you analyze. The listing text you paste or the numbers you enter, and the report DealTough produces, so you can find them again under “Your recent deals.”
  • Photos you add. Sent for analysis of condition and scam signals while your report is made. We do not keep the photos after that.
  • Plan and usage. Your plan, how many analyses you have used, and your credit balance.
  • Basic technical logs. IP address, browser type and error details, kept for security and debugging.

We do not store card numbers. Payments run through Stripe, which handles card details directly.

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it with advertisers.

Shared reports

If you choose to share a report, anyone with that link can view it. Nothing is shared unless you ask for a link.

Cookies

DealTough uses one cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Account details and saved deals are kept while your account exists. Technical logs are kept for a short period for debugging. Ask us to delete your account and we’ll remove your account record and your saved deals.

Your choices

Email support@doertoughmikeai.com from the address on your account to get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account.

Payments and tax information

Stripe receives the contact, billing address, payment, and tax information you provide at checkout, together with order and subscription details needed to process purchases, prevent fraud, manage refunds, and meet tax obligations. For purchases sold through Link, Stripe acts as merchant of record. See Stripe’s Privacy Policy. We receive payment status and billing records, but not your full card number.
