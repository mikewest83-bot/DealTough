1. What DealTough is

DealTough estimates what a used item is worth, scores the asking price, and suggests an opening offer and a walk-away price. Its reports are generated automatically from the information you give it and from comparable listings. It is not a professional appraisal, inspection, or legal or financial advice.

2. How estimates work

Market values are estimated from comparable listings currently for sale, mainly on eBay, adjusted for condition. Listings show what sellers are asking, not always what items sell for, and results depend on how well the listing describes the item. Check anything that matters — condition, title, history, and the item itself — before you buy. You are responsible for your purchase decisions.
